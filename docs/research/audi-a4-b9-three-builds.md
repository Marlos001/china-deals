# Audi A4 B9: three complete build directions

28 October 2026. 2018–2019 Ambiente sedan, 2.0T FWD. Standard bumper assumed; verify VIN, engine, factory lights and body hardware. Every modification is optional unless required by a chosen part or installation. Chinese listing prices are observations, not verified checkout or landed quotes.

## Three coordinated looks

### Silver surfer

- **Body:** Silver metallic body; paint-match by VIN code and spray-out card. Satin metallic accents, not chrome everywhere.
- **Wheels:** Brushed silver or bright satin 19-inch multi-spoke / split-five-spoke; subtle center cap.
- **Front:** Body-color bumper and lip, satin silver grille blades; black inner honeycomb and sensors.
- **Side:** Body-color mirror caps and side skirts, satin window trim; dark neutral glass within local rules.
- **Rear:** Body-color low lip spoiler and diffuser surround; satin silver exhaust tips.
- **Cabin:** Black or charcoal interior with brushed aluminum trim and restrained white ambient light.

### All black

- **Body:** Deep black metallic or gloss black; paint-match existing body, protect high-contact areas.
- **Wheels:** Satin black 19-inch split five or multi-spoke; satin graphite improves spoke visibility.
- **Front:** Gloss black grille and rings; body-color bumper with gloss or satin black lip, not a different black on every piece.
- **Side:** Gloss black window trim and mirrors; body-color skirts with satin black lower blade.
- **Rear:** Body-color lip spoiler, black diffuser and dark stainless tips. Keep lights legal and unobscured.
- **Cabin:** Black trim with subtle dark aluminum and white ambient light.

### Dark blue + black

- **Body:** Deep metallic navy body, matched to existing paint or a professionally applied wrap.
- **Wheels:** Satin graphite or black 19-inch multi-spoke; machined details optional.
- **Front:** Gloss black grille, rings and lower lip; blue bumper, black openings.
- **Side:** Blue mirror caps and skirts with black lower blades and gloss black window trim.
- **Rear:** Blue lip spoiler, gloss black diffuser and black or dark stainless tips.
- **Cabin:** Black and brushed dark aluminum, subtle cool white or blue ambient light.

## Custom 19-inch wheels

Start a supplier request around a square 19×8.5 5×112 wheel and a 245/35 R19 tyre as **candidates to validate**, not approved final specifications. Confirm the current wheel stamp, tyre placard, bore (commonly 66.5/66.6 mm), offset, brake template, hub, bolt-seat and thread, load/speed rating, tyre clearance through full steering and suspension travel, and applicable Brazilian rules. Offset requires vehicle-specific measurement; do not order by model name alone. Ask for drawing and per-wheel load test documentation linked to the exact wheel, size and manufacturing process. Store ratings do not verify wheel safety or forged construction.

## Full parts checklist

### Wheels & stance

- **4 × custom 19-inch wheels — All:** Request exact CAD, PCD, center bore, offset, load and brake clearance; choose finish by build.
- **4 × matched tyres — All:** A matched 245/35 R19 is a research starting point only; confirm placard, load/speed rating, clearance and local approval.
- **Correct wheel bolts and locking set — All:** Match bolt seat, thread, engagement and wheel manufacturer specifications; do not assume factory bolts fit.
- **Metal valve stems, balancing and alignment — All:** Match wheel valves and TPMS approach to the car; align after installation.
- **Center caps, hub rings if required — Optional:** Prefer correct hub bore; rings require correct material and accurate fit.
- **Springs or coilovers and alignment — Optional:** Only matched FWD axle loads; preserve damper travel and daily comfort. Avoid spacers unless professionally engineered.

### Front styling

- **Correct pre-facelift standard-bumper grille — All:** Black inner structure plus chosen accent; verify PDC, camera, radar and number-plate brackets.
- **Paint-matched front lip or splitter — Optional:** Standard-bumper mounting geometry; get primer/material and ground-clearance details.
- **Fog grilles / lower air guides — Optional:** Left and right inserts; match existing sensors and equipment.
- **Front bumper conversion and hardware — Optional:** Only if changing design: bumper skin, brackets, grilles, undertray, sensors, fogs, paint and fitting.
- **Rings, model badges and plate surround — Optional:** Select satin silver or gloss black; do not claim S/RS or quattro specification.
- **Headlights and modules — Optional:** Confirm original halogen/xenon/LED, left-hand-drive beam, modules, harness, coding and legal approval; pair vs one.

### Side styling

- **Paint-matched side skirts and end caps — Optional:** Verify sedan and pre-facelift, attachment clips and door clearance.
- **Mirror caps — Optional:** Use original mounts; verify heating, indicators and camera provisions.
- **Window-trim finish or wrap — Optional:** Satin silver for surfer; black for the darker builds.
- **Door handle covers or refinish — Optional:** Preserve keyless sensor function and paint match.
- **Fender trim and mud flaps — Optional:** Subtle OEM+; check mounting and bodywork before drilling.
- **Glass tint — Optional:** Use locally permitted light transmission; keep lighting and visibility compliant.

### Rear styling

- **Trunk lip spoiler and fitting tape — Recommended:** Confirm sedan deck shape, base surface, paintable material and mounting; color-match or restrained contrast.
- **Diffuser / rear valance — Optional:** Standard versus S-line bumper and exhaust outlet configuration must match.
- **Exhaust tips and hangers — Optional:** Match outlet layout; avoid nonfunctional covers and heat damage.
- **Rear bumper conversion — Optional:** Include brackets, reflectors, sensors, diffuser, trim and paint; confirm pre-facelift sedan.
- **Tail lights / harness / coding — Optional:** Confirm sedan body, inner+outer set, LHD market, lamp modules and connector; no smoke film over required lamps.
- **Rear rings, badges, reflectors — Optional:** Finish to match theme and retain legal reflectors.

### Cabin

- **Steering wheel, trim or retrim — Optional:** Airbag, buttons, heating and wiring must match; specialist installation.
- **Trim wraps / console inserts — Optional:** Brushed aluminum for silver; darker satin for other builds. Avoid airbag seams.
- **Ambient lighting — Optional:** Use reversible fused harness and subdued white/blue light; avoid interfering with safety controls.
- **Pedals, mats and sill plates — Optional:** Pedals must be secure and mats must not obstruct movement.

### Performance & reliability

- **Service baseline and diagnostic scan — First:** Oil, plugs, coils as needed, cooling, leaks, brakes and transmission health before tuning.
- **Tyres, pads, brake fluid — First:** Quality tyres, correct pad compound and fresh fluid deliver useful daily performance.
- **ECU stage 1 and logs — Optional:** Local reputable calibrator; match exact engine/ECU, fuel and gearbox limits; inspect legal and insurance effects.
- **TCU calibration — Conditional:** Only if exact gearbox and tuner support require it; verify clutch and transmission health.
- **Intake or high-flow panel filter — Optional:** JTLD is a research lead only; confirm engine, MAF and housing; gains vary.
- **Intercooler and hoses — Conditional:** Consider with logged intake temperatures and sustained demand; exact engine fit and pressure testing.
- **Resonated catback — Optional:** Confirm FWD fit, noise limits, exhaust routing and shipping; retain emissions equipment.
- **Turbo upgrade and supporting parts — Later:** Turbo ID, manifold, oil/coolant lines, fueling and custom tune must be confirmed; stock turbo fits moderate first stage.
- **Engine and transmission mounts — Optional:** Fit exact drivetrain; increased vibration can hurt daily comfort.
- **Downpipe / catalyst changes — Avoid for this brief:** Legal and emissions implications; retain approved emissions equipment.

### Finishing & installation

- **Primer, paint, clear coat and test panels — All:** Use vehicle paint code, spray-out, blending and material-compatible adhesion promoter.
- **Clips, rivets, brackets and adhesive — All:** Obtain item-specific mounting kits; account for broken clips.
- **Protective film, ceramic coating and detail — Optional:** Protect freshly cured paint; follow paint-shop curing guidance.
- **Professional fit, calibration and alignment — All:** Budget installation, sensor calibration, wheel balancing, shipping and import charges separately.
