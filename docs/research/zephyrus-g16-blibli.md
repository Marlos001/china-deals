## Access and account limits

The [Blibli HTTP probes](../../.local/guest-research/blibli/access-probe.json) record 403 responses and the [browser evidence](../../.local/guest-research/blibli/t3-browser-evidence.json) records a verification/challenge page. These establish the observed access result for those attempts, not a universal restriction on every user or network. Native filters, live offers and checkout were not verified. The broader [guest search record](../../.local/guest-research/g16-search-v1.json) returned no ranked offers and reports blocked searches; its empty result is not evidence of no inventory.

Whether a foreign user can register, what phone verification is required, whether an account offers discounts or access benefits, and whether any particular forwarding agent can purchase from Indonesia are **unknown** on the retained evidence. An account benefit has not been proven; neither has its absence. No authorization status is established for any seller. Before purchase, independently check the exact listing and variant, current price and availability, seller and warranty, payment and account requirements, and delivery arrangements.
