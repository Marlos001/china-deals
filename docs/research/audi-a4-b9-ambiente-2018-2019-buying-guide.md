# Audi A4 B9 Ambiente 2018–2019: OEM+ parts shortlist

**Researched 28 October 2026. Target: Brazilian Ambiente sedan, 2.0 TFSI, FWD; standard bumper unless the car has already been converted. Delivery references: CEP 12242-160 or 05531-040, São Paulo.** Style: comfortable daily driver with a moderately sporty appearance and response.

**My build choice:** genuine 18-inch wheels with good tyres, a small gloss-black boot spoiler, a subtle splitter for the **standard** bumper, and a conservative locally supported tune only after identifying the engine/ECU. Keep the factory turbo and working original lights. Buy the grille on verified fit rather than the lowest advertised price. A full bumper conversion, unknown-brand wheels and a big turbo are poor first purchases for this brief.

This is a prioritized buying/research shortlist, **not a cart of fully verified delivered offers**. Prices below distinguish fresh visible pages from cached references; sellers' stock claims are not checkout verification. No complete Brazil-delivered quote was obtained for either CEP. Unknown seller metrics and fitment remain explicit. No sellers were contacted, accounts used or purchases made.

### Measurements that cannot be skipped

- **PCD: 5×112.** B9 big-bore applications are commonly described as **66.5/66.6 mm**; obtain the wheel manufacturer's exact centre-bore drawing and compatibility. Do not round a physically undersized bore up. A3-style **57.1 mm** wheels are not a direct B9 hub fit simply because they share 5×112. See [LFI's B8/B9 fitment guide](https://lf-ind.com/pages/audi-a4-s4-rs4-allroad-b9-fitment-guide).
- Confirm width, offset, wheel load rating, brake clearance, valve/TPMS arrangement, bolt thread, bolt-seat profile and bolt length. APEX specifies **OEM R13 ball-seat hardware for its documented VS-5 application**; that does not apply to every aftermarket wheel.
- Use four compatible rolling diameters and the required tyre ratings. Relative to 225/50 R17, nominal 245/40 R18 diameter changes about **−0.55%** and 245/35 R19 about **−0.41%** (geometry only; not a regulatory or clearance approval).
- Do not import a generic forged-wheel listing without a drawing, per-wheel load evidence and test documentation tied to the exact specification. For used OE wheels, request inner-barrel photos, runout inspection and repair/weld history.

## 7. BRL, freight and taxes for the two São Paulo CEPs

### Reference rates and tax basis

Freshly retrieved [ECB daily reference feed](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/eurofxref/eurofxref-daily.xml), **rate date 27 October 2026**: **US$1 = R$5.34744; £1 = R$7.12711; €1 = R$5.96240; ¥1 = R$0.80201**. These exclude payment spread/IOF and are not customs PTAX.

São Paulo's [RICMS Annex II, Article 80](https://legislacao.fazenda.sp.gov.br/Paginas/RICMS---Anexo-II---Redu%C3%A7%C3%B5es-de-Base-de-C%C3%A1lculo---Artigo-80.aspx) provides an effective **17%** burden for qualifying RTS postal/express imports, with the retained provision effective through **31 December 2026**. Both supplied CEPs are treated as São Paulo destinations; actual last-mile freight differs and was not quoted.

For **non-PRC qualifying RTS** purchases, the [Receita manual](https://www.gov.br/receitafederal/pt-br/assuntos/aduana-e-comercio-exterior/manuais/remessas-postal-e-expressa/preciso-pagar-impostos-nas-compras-internacionais/quais-sao-os-impostos-devidos) specifies **60% import duty**, then ICMS gross-up. Goods + freight + insurance form the customs base. Thus an indicative 17% scenario is `(goods + freight + insurance in BRL) × 1.60 / 0.83`, before separate fees. Applicability is not verified by this calculation.

For eligible **PRC** transactions, current Receita text instead gives **0% federal duty up to US$50**, and **60% less US$30 above US$50**. The transaction must actually qualify. Do not automatically treat Alibaba, an agent-forwarded parcel, or an AliExpress link without a tax-confirmed checkout as PRC. RTS's US$3,000 customs-value limit and shipment eligibility still apply. See [Receita's worked examples](https://www.gov.br/receitafederal/pt-br/assuntos/aduana-e-comercio-exterior/manuais/remessas-postal-e-expressa/preciso-pagar-impostos-nas-compras-internacionais/quanto-pagarei-de-imposto).

## 9. Purchase order I would follow

1. Confirm the actual wheel stamps, tyre placard, bumper and engine/ECU codes; establish the maintenance baseline.
2. Find a genuine, inspected **18-inch OE set** locally; choose tyres for wet-road grip and comfort.
3. Add **CT834 spoiler** if its all-in quote remains attractive; then the **AU-A4-B9-FD2G** splitter if clearance and return terms work.
4. Refinish or replace the grille only with a verified standard-bumper, pre-facelift fit; retain factory lights unless there is a fault or a properly scoped retrofit.
5. Get a written, engine-specific local calibration proposal with before/after logging and aftercare. Keep the airbox/turbo initially.
6. Revisit intake, cooling and quiet exhaust based on actual use and quotes. A hybrid turbo and a full body conversion stay outside the first round.
